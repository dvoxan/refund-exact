Refund Exact - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Refund Exact ("the app") is operated by Lightscordless LLC ("we", "us"). It helps Shopify merchants work out the exact refund amount for a partial return on a discounted order. This policy explains what the app accesses and how it is handled.

What the app accesses

When a merchant looks up an order, the app reads that order's number, line items (product title, variant, quantity, price), discounts, tax lines and financial status from the Shopify Admin API. The app does not request or read customer names, email addresses, phone numbers or addresses.

What we store

We store only the merchant's shop domain and the access token Shopify issues to the app, so the app can keep working between visits. We do not store orders, order contents or any customer data. Order data is read on request, used to calculate the refund, shown to the merchant, and then discarded.

How data is used and shared

Data is used only to calculate and display the refund amount to the merchant who asked. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or analytics. The app is hosted on Render (render.com), which processes requests on our behalf and may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses and timestamps.

Retention and deletion

When a merchant uninstalls the app, or Shopify sends a shop erasure request, we delete the stored access token for that shop. Because we hold no customer data, requests from Shopify to view or erase customer data have nothing to return or delete. The app responds to these requests as Shopify requires.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Requests are verified with Shopify session tokens, and webhooks are verified with Shopify's HMAC signature.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Since the app holds no customer data, this mostly means the shop access token, which is removed when you uninstall. You can also contact us at any time.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
